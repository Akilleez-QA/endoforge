# Loop 1 precommit (v1, finalised before any step-3 run; sha256 in receipt/precommit.sha256 and in the hand-off)

Written by the loop-1 session on 2026-10-17/18 after step 0 (freeze), step 1 (deviation step) and step 2 (criteria, audit, canary dry-run), before any noise-floor or comparison run. The machine was updated and rebooted between the canary dry-run and the first noise-floor run (kernel 7.2.5 to 7.2.8, receipt/freeze.json tools); the A/A replicate therefore measures the noise floor on the state every comparison run shares. Everything below is Proposed until the receipts say otherwise. Labels follow docs/CONVENTIONS.md.

## 1. Hypotheses

- **H1 (whole stack, the charter's).** Moving the whole stack (S-new: stub -> contract-stage persona with fold-in -> blind evaluator with property-instantiated tests; G-new: two blind Sonnet candidates with compile and schema self-check and one Opus escalation; Q-new: G1-G7 plus G8 range enforcement; R-new: no output persona stage; retrieval over the loop-0 library) raises clean promotion on the 20 working requests against the incumbent (factory v2 + low effort, single-shot form) by at least +6 of 20 under the median panel rule, with guardrails intact.
- **H2 (noise floor).** Two identical incumbent runs on the 20 development requests differ in clean count by at most 3 of 20 (P = 0.77, the charter's prediction); above 6 of 20 the loop stops and resizes.
- **H3 (named cross-stage interaction).** Blind generation (G-new) helps more under S-new's explicit stub than under the incumbent's contract: |S x G interaction| >= 0.15 with the sign favouring (S-new, G-new).
- **H4 (effort).** The incumbent's low-effort move costs nothing on clean: the default-effort arm is within 3 of 20 of the low-effort mean.
- **H5 (deviation arm, D-02).** Folding a blind output reviewer's named change into one bounded repair cycle (amended stub -> re-evaluated frozen spec -> one blind candidate -> one re-review) raises clean promotion over the unrepaired all-new artefact by at least +3 of 20 (receipt/deviations.md; the critic's expected gain +4.5, P(>= +6) = 0.40). The challenger that faces the incumbent is E = all-new + D-02; B (all-new without the repair) is kept and scored so D-02's own effect is readable.

## 2. The design, exactly as run

- **Units.** Configuration manifest (receipt/manifest-incumbent.json, receipt/manifest-challenger.json; every brief hashed in receipt/briefs.sha256; gate.mjs and the other fixed files hashed in receipt/freeze.json) x request, paired by request.
- **Subjects.** Development set = the 20 loop-0 primary requests (noise floor only). Working set = 20 of the 40 reserve (stratified split, seed 20261018, receipt/freeze.json). Sealed set = the other 20, run once at the end under both manifests, never tuned on; no optimiser agent sees their text (they are read only by the factory stages that build from them and by the sealed oracles).
- **Step 3, noise floor.** inc-low twice on the development set (the default-effort third arm is carried forward by the budget decision below): 40 runs in request-complete blocks, the two replicates shuffled within each block, blocks shuffled (seed 20261118), four runs in flight so the replicates interleave in time (receipt/noise-plan.json). Every gated artefact is graded by the panel.
- **Step 5, the screen (as budgeted).** Per working request three artefacts: A = incumbent run (S-old, G-old, shared context as in loop 0; its own output persona stage); B = S-new -> G-new, then one blind output-persona review (recorded, used by E and by the R filter); E = B + D-02 (if B's reviewers accepted outright, E is B; otherwise the amended stub, a re-evaluated frozen spec, one blind candidate with the self-checks and Opus escalation, and one fresh blind re-review). The corners C (S-new -> G-old) and D (S-old -> G-new) and the post-hoc Q/R factor reading are carried forward; this loop reads three paired contrasts, not stage main effects. Requests in a seeded order (seed 20261518), three in flight, request-complete blocks; minimum 12 requests analysed if the budget stops the screen early. G8 is computed on every gated artefact and reported (Q-new's effect on the clean count is readable as a filter on B and E but is not part of the primary rule).
- **Panel.** oracle-sonnet (Sonnet 5.5, technician), oracle-opus (Opus 5.5, reviewer), oracle-codex (Codex GPT-6.1, technician, reasoning high, protocol v1.1: no user config, no web search, named model, a no-tools rule). Each wrote its criteria for the 40 development and working requests in step 2 before any loop-1 artefact existed (hashes in receipt/oracle-criteria-summary.json; a five-sheet anchoring audit scored 2, 1, 1, 2, 1 of 2, receipt/criteria-audit.json); sealed-set criteria are written in step 6 before scoring. Artefact sheets are anonymised, shuffled with a seed, batched six distinct requests per call, graded against the oracle's own criteria with scoring briefs v2: the verdict follows from Q1-Q3 (clean needs yes/yes/yes and no required change; any no is not-usable; otherwise usable-with-change) and the oracle's stated verdict can only make it more severe. This rule is the slow-loop revision made after canary dry-run attempt 1 failed (CC-011 flagged by one oracle of three, the technician frames having graded the quote checker against their own criteria that ask for a quote); attempt 2 under the revised briefs passed 3/3 with no filler false-rejected (receipt/canary-dryrun.jsonl, receipt/oracle-protocol-revisions.jsonl). No optimiser agent reads anything under work/oracle/.
- **Clean rules.** Primary (median): the artefact is gated by G1-G7 on the coordinator's run, promoted by its manifest's rule (A and E: gated and no persona reject, pending-change ships; B: the same rule, with B's single review), and the median of the three oracle verdicts is clean. Strict: all three clean. Lenient: none not-usable. All three recorded; adoption reads the median rule. G8 is reported as a secondary filter, not applied in the primary rule (both manifests are compared on G1-G7 promotion so that the gate addition's effect is read separately).
- **Scores.** Paired by request: improved / worsened counts with an exact sign test; the difference of proportions with a paired-bootstrap 95 % interval; Clopper-Pearson intervals on every rate; **primary contrast E versus A; registered secondary E versus B (D-02's effect); exploratory B versus A**; inter-oracle exact, within-one and kappa agreement; wall as paired log differences with bootstrap intervals; tokens per request per stage from receipt/tokens.jsonl; repair frequency and repair resolution per request.

## 3. Acceptance, guardrails, stop rules

- **Adopt the challenger E** only if: paired clean gain (E versus A) >= +6 of 20 under the median rule; canaries 3/3 (receipt/canary-dryrun.json); inter-oracle within-one agreement >= 80 % for every pair over the working-set artefacts; and every guardrail holds. Below +6 is "no evidence at this n", not "worse". A guardrail breach rejects the bundle, not its parts.
- **Guardrails (pre-registered bounds, challenger versus incumbent on the working set):** G1-G7 pass count >= incumbent - 3 of 20; G5 determinism and G6 bounded time 100 % of gated artefacts; median per-request wall on the critical path (spec then generation) <= 2.0 x the incumbent's median run wall; tokens per request (all stages, no scoring) <= 3.0 x the incumbent's; canary recall 3/3. Efficiency (tokens per clean promotion) ranks non-inferior challengers and never decides adoption alone.
- **Stop rules.** Hard stop at 12,000,000 metered tokens (the runner exits; the hand-off is written). After the A/A: |delta clean| > 6 of 20 -> stop and resize. Budget decision at step 4 by the rule in §4. The screen stops early only at a request-complete block boundary and only with >= 12 requests complete.
- **Rollback.** The incumbent is never modified; the challenger is a separate manifest; nothing under campaigns/x1-spec-study/ changes.

## 4. Budget decision (taken at the end of step 2, with the arithmetic; re-checked after step 3)

Metered spend at the end of step 2 was about 2.9M tokens (receipt/tokens-summary.json), 1.3M of it five Codex criteria batches that ran web searches at a full-context round each before the Codex protocol was fixed (v1.1: no user config, no search, named model, a no-tools rule; measured 21k per batch after). Smoke figures (tokens, all kinds): incumbent run 26k; S-new 25.6k; G-new (width 2 with escalation) 30k; G-old on S-new 19.4k; output persona 6k; D-02 repair cycle about 45k when triggered; scoring about 56k per batch of six sheets across the three oracles (about 9k per artefact). Projection with the draft's four-artefact screen, the three-arm noise floor and the sealed set exceeded the 12M hard stop (about 12.4M). The precommitted choice is therefore the smallest design that keeps the charter's primary contrast and the deviation arm readable on all 20 working requests: noise floor = the A/A pair (40 runs, about 1.0M plus 0.37M scoring); screen = A, B, E on 20 requests (about 20 x (26 + 25.6 + 30 + 6 + 0.7 x 45) = 2.4M plus 60 artefacts' scoring 0.55M); sealed = A, B, E on 20 (about 2.4M plus 0.55M); use, audit, analysis about 0.3M: about 10.5M projected in all, under the hard stop, over the 8M target (recorded as a miss of the target, with the cause). **Re-check after step 3:** if the measured step-3 cost per run plus scoring projects the loop above 11.5M, the sealed set is reduced to A and E (B's sealed artefacts not generated), then to the first 12 sealed requests in seeded order; the screen is never reduced below 20 requests x {A, B, E} unless the hard stop is hit, in which case the completed request-complete blocks (>= 12) are analysed and the stop is recorded. If instead the projection lands under 9M, corner C (S-new -> G-old, about 0.5M with scoring) is added to the screen for the G main effect given S-new.

## 5. Forecasts

receipt/forecasts.json: 27 forecasts with probabilities, seven of them marked unobservable this loop because their corners or arm were carried forward (scored null, excluded from the mean, kept for loop 2). The named cross-stage interaction hypothesis (F08) is one of those; the critic's interaction hypothesis (contract-time review and output repair are substitutes for some intent defects and complements for defects revealed by actual calls) is observed descriptively through repair frequency under S-new.

## 6. Honesty rules observed

Optimiser and generator agents never read oracle briefs, criteria, the sealed set or the canaries (work/oracle/ and work/canaries/ are read only by the coordinator's scoring code and by the sealed oracles). Results are recorded as they fall (receipt/runs.jsonl, scores-*.jsonl, tokens.jsonl append-only). No best-of-N: the first passing candidate is selected by fixed order; every run counts. A difference is a win only above the measured noise floor. Forecasts were written before the first noise-floor run. The briefs and the kit libraries do not change after this hash (their hashes are in receipt/briefs.sha256 and receipt/freeze.json); a change would be a new version with a recorded reason and a new precommit hash. Three changes were made before the hash and after the smoke runs, none after a scored run: the artefact-folder layout (gate G7's id rule), the Codex protocol v1.1, and the sheet renderer's refusal text (one trap message chosen by the argument at fault, equal for every arm).
