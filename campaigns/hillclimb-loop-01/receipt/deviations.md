The critic was a Codex CLI agent (GPT-6.1, reasoning high) in a sealed read-only directory holding the spec, the errata, the method, the factory protocol, the lessons log, the loop-0 receipts, P-04b and the loop-1 design draft; its full output is `deviations-critic-raw.md` (call and input hashes in `deviations-critic.json`; 739k metered tokens, 20 file reads, 13.7k output, 316 s). The orchestrator session separately proposed D-08 (`campaigns/x1-spec-study/receipt/ORCHESTRATOR-TO-LOOP1-01.md`). This file is the loop-1 session's ranking and decision. Numbers from the critic are its own prospective, subjective forecasts (Proposed); the loop-0 figures it cites are Recorded (probe).

## Ranking by expected gain per cost (the critic's, checked against the loop's own budget)

1. D-02 bounded repair (expected +4.5 of 20; +17-23k per request).
2. D-09 decision-stable defaults (+3; +0.8k).
3. D-12 invariant packs (+1.4; -3k on covered requests, packs unpriced).
4. D-07 one readable contrast (0; -18 % loop tokens).
5. D-04 declarative workflows (+1.5; +6k plus renderer).
6. D-11 typed fixtures (+0.6; +0.6k). 7. D-06 compiled glue (+0.2; -2k). 8. D-10 conditional width (-0.2; -8.7k).

The loop-1 session agrees with the top choice. D-02 is the deviation that attacks the largest observed backlog (15 of 18 shipped capabilities were pending a named change) and the one hc-01's raters pointed at; it is implementable with the stages that already exist; and its cost fits once the screen is reduced.

## Decision: D-02 enters loop 1 as arm E

- **Arm E = all-new + D-02.** Every gated all-new artefact (B) gets one blind output-persona review (the same call the R filter needs). If every persona accepts, E is B unchanged. If any persona names a change or rejects, the named change becomes an amendment: the stub is revised once with the finding folded in, the blind evaluator rewrites the contract and tests against the revised stub (a new frozen spec; the old one is kept), one replacement candidate is generated blind (width 1, compile and schema self-check, Opus escalation on gate failure), gated, and reviewed once more by a fresh blind persona call. E's promotion rule is the incumbent's (gated and no persona reject; a remaining accept-with-change ships as pending-change, so the clean credit can only come from the panel finding the repaired artefact clean). Repair frequency, repair success and the second review's verdicts are recorded per request.
- **Primary contrast (the charter's, kept):** E versus A, paired by request: the whole stack including its deviation against the incumbent. **Registered secondary:** E versus B, D-02's own effect on the same base artefacts. **Exploratory:** B versus A.
- **What was dropped, and why (budget).** Metered spend before step 2 finished was about 2.6M tokens, 1.3M of it Codex criteria batches that ran web searches at a full-context round each (stopped, archived, rewritten without tools; `tokens.jsonl` keeps every row). Projected with the four-artefact screen, the third noise-floor arm and the sealed set, the loop exceeded the 12M hard stop. The step-4 rule in PRECOMMIT.md therefore selects: noise floor = the A/A pair only (the default-effort arm carried forward); screen = A, B and E on all 20 working requests (the S-new -> G-old and S-old -> G-new corners carried forward, so stage main effects and the S x G interaction are not estimated this loop); sealed = A and E. This is D-07's "one readable contrast" in effect, which the critic ranked fourth on its own but which the budget imposes.
- **Honesty.** The output persona in E is a production agent; it reads the artefact sheet and nothing from the oracle. The oracle scores E's final artefact blind, as it scores every other.

## Carried forward to loop 2 (ranked)
